Paywall placement
Shown on "Create your first group →" tap, before the form. The upsell shows real value (voting, grocery list, 200+ recipes) before asking for money. 7-day free trial lowers friction. V1 decision: free tier does not exist — subscribe or browse recipes only.
Two fields only
Group name + meals per week. Nothing else. Emoji picker, group photo, dietary restrictions — all deferred. The threshold rule (task #30) triggers on the second member joining, not at creation.
Four share surfaces
Messages, Email, Share link (iOS share sheet), QR code. The join link is a universal link — tapping it opens the app directly to the join screen if installed, or the App Store if not. Task #43.
Link expiry
Join links expire after 7 days or after first use, whichever comes first. The group settings screen has a "Generate new invite link" option for re-inviting. This prevents old links floating around.
Recipient doesn't need to subscribe
The person accepting an invite is a member of an existing group — they don't need their own subscription. The subscription is per-group, owned by the creator. This is the right model: couples share one subscription, not two.

Invite nudge on detail
If a group has only one member, the detail screen shows the "Planning solo?" banner above the history. Disappears once a second member joins. Not shown in the mock above — shown when Claire hasn't joined yet.
Menu history as social proof
Past menus with recipe thumbnail collages are the visual anchor of the group detail. They serve as a record and a reminder of what worked. Tapping a past menu shows a read-only version of the menu detail from flow-03.
Threshold exposed in settings
Approval threshold (how many votes needed to confirm a recipe) is a group setting, not a global default. For a 2-person group it defaults to 2 (unanimous). For a 4-person group it defaults to 3 (majority). The owner can lower it — useful for larger households where unanimous agreement is unrealistic.
Action sheet for destructive actions
Remove member and Leave group use iOS-native action sheet styling (not a modal or inline confirm). The confirm option is red; Cancel is always present and prominent. This matches the system pattern users expect for irreversible actions.
Design decisions
Subscription is per-group, owned by creator
The person who creates the group pays. Members who join via invite don't need a subscription of their own. This mirrors how most family plan products work and removes a significant barrier to adoption — Claire shouldn't need to enter her credit card to vote on dinner. For groups with a natural "organizer" (one partner, a parent in a family), this maps to social reality.
Group management lives in settings, not a dedicated screen
The Groups tab from v1 IA is gone. Groups are accessed via the group pill on the Home screen → group detail → settings. For V1 with its 1–2 group per user assumption, there's no need for a top-level groups list. If multi-group use grows (someone in a household group and a friend group), revisit — but don't design for that yet.
Open: free trial vs. free tier
Current design: 7-day free trial, then $4.99/mo. Alternative: permanent free tier (1 group, read-only recipe library, no voting) with a paid upgrade. The free tier preserves discoverability but splits the product experience. The trial is cleaner and respects the product's core value — if voting and planning is the product, gating behind a trial is honest. Decide before TestFlight.
Open: what happens to the group when the owner cancels?
If the subscriber cancels, the group becomes read-only: members can see history and the recipe library but can't create new menus or vote. The group is not deleted — resubscribing restores it fully. This is the least destructive policy and avoids punishing members for the owner's billing decision. The "leave group" option lets any member exit regardless of subscription status.
✓ As Built — invite sharing surface
Invite sharing lives in GroupMembersScreen as an _InviteSection widget. Auto-calls POST /groups/:id/invites (get-or-create) on load. Shows a copyable menuapp://join/{token} URL pill, expiry time formatted as "in Xd" / "in Xh", a "Share invite" button (iOS share sheet via share_plus), and a "↻ New link" refresh button. Not yet a separate screen after group creation — it's discoverable via the Members tab.
✓ As Built — token management
Backend: POST /groups/:id/invites returns the existing non-expired token or creates one (7-day expiry). POST /groups/:id/invites/refresh replaces it with a new one. The frontend refresh button lets the group owner rotate the link without going to settings.
✓ As Built — Groups tab added
A Groups tab exists in the 4-tab shell (Home / Groups / Recipes / Shop). Groups are accessible both from the Home screen group pill and directly from the tab. The "group management lives in settings only" design decision was revised — the tab is the primary entry point.
✓ As Built — join endpoint
POST /invites/:token/join handles membership. Returns 200 with group_id on success, 409 if already a member, 410 if token expired, 404 if not found. The mobile InviteCubit maps each status to a typed InviteErrorKind with a context-appropriate message and action.
